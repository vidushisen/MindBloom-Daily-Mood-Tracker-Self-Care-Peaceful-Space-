The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Method

Whenever you feel panicked, overwhelmed, or detached — follow this interactive sensory step-by-step tool to anchor back into the present moment.

5

Look Around: 5 Things You Can SEE

Notice 5 objects around you right now (e.g., a shadow, a cup, a pattern on the wall, the sky).

4

Feel Around: 4 Things You Can TOUCH

Feel the fabric of your clothes, the coolness of the table, or your feet grounded on the floor.

3

Listen Closely: 3 Things You Can HEAR

Listen beyond the obvious: distant traffic, fan humming, bird chirping, your own breath.

2

Inhale Gently: 2 Things You Can SMELL

Notice any scent in the air, your perfume, coffee, or fresh air from the window.

1

Acknowledge: 1 Thing You Can TASTE or Love About Yourself

Notice the taste in your mouth, take a sip of water, or say one kind thing to yourself.
